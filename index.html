About me

I am Chengyan Deng, a master’s student in Control Engineering at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), advised by Prof. Li Yu. I expect to graduate in June 2027 and received my B.Eng. in Automation from Hefei University of Technology.

My research interests lie in image and video generation, diffusion models, and image super-resolution, with a focus on efficient generative models and high-quality image reconstruction.

I am currently a research intern at Huawei Noah’s Ark Lab, exploring Dense-to-MoE methods for text-to-image generation. I am also fortunate to work as a research assistant at Nanjing University under the guidance of Prof. Kai Zhang. Previously, I was a research intern at OPPO AI Research Center.
